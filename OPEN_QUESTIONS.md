# Open questions

Everything that could not be closed from a primary source. The dominant cause is
mechanical: BOE's consolidated texts of long codes (Codigo Civil, Codigo Penal, LECrim,
LEC, LGT, RDLeg 7/2015) truncate before the relevant article when fetched. Someone with
BOE open in a browser can close most of this in about an hour.

Ordered by how much it blocks.
