Keeping the accounts healthy

New accounts that only ever post links get restricted quickly, so please use each account the way a person would. Every day, spend about ten minutes on each one: read the groups it has joined, like and comment on a few posts where you have something useful to say, and reply to comments and messages. If you have a real conversation with someone in a group, it is fine to send them a friend request. Please do not send friend requests to random strangers, because that is exactly what Facebook treats as spam.

Some groups ask a few questions before they let you in. Answer them honestly. Some take a day or two to accept you. Keep asking each day until they do.

The Dutch groups on Account 7 have not all been checked yet. Before joining one, open it and make sure it is active and about living in Spain. If it is not, skip it and tell me.

Each posting day

1. Open the day's email.
2. For each language, log in to the account named for it.
3. Open each group link, paste the text exactly as it is, and add that language's image.
4. Note what happened in the Excel sheet I am sharing with you, and paste the link to each post.

Please do not change the wording or the link, and do not add hashtags or emojis. Each language links to its own page, so the links have to stay as they are.

If a group holds the post for an admin to approve, write "Waiting for admin" and move on. If a post is removed, or an admin writes to you, do not post in that group again and let me know. If someone asks a question under a post, send it to me and I will give you the reply.

Reddit and forums

There are also some Reddit communities and forums we will use later. Please do not post there yet. I will tell you when, and how.

If anything is unclear, just reply to this email and ask. Thank you.
