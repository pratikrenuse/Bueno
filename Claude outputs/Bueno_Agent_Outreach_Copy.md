# Bueno estate agent outreach: sequence copy

Three messages. Email 1 asks permission, Email 2 is the single follow-up, Email 3 is sent
only to agents who answer yes. No Bueno sales pitch in Email 1 or 2, because the offer is
the checklist and the answers, not the subscription.

Merge fields: `{{first_name}}`, `{{company_name}}`, `{{town}}`. Every merge field has a
fallback, because 3,481 of the 5,426 contacts have no town and 3,512 have no first name.

**Subject:** Here is the checklist

Hello {{first_name|there}},

The checklist is attached. Your buyer can work through it before signing, and you are
welcome to forward it under your own name.

Two things it covers that catch people out most often. Where the seller is a non-resident
without a permanent establishment, the buyer withholds 3 percent of the price and pays it
to the tax office with Modelo 211 within one month. It is a payment on account of the
seller's tax, not an extra cost and not the seller's final rate. Separately, property sale
and purchase intermediaries are obliged entities under the Spanish anti-money-laundering
regime in their own right, so that file does not sit with the bank.

We also run a small WhatsApp group for agents who deal with foreign buyers. When a client
asks something you cannot answer, you ask it there and we answer it. If you would like the
