# Presenter photo library: 50 prompts

Attach 2 or 3 of the finalised photos as the reference images every time (the front-facing beige blazer photo, the cafe photo and the seaside photo work best together). Paste the identity block first, then one scene.

## Identity block (paste before every scene)

Use the attached reference photos. Keep this exact same woman, with the same face, bone structure, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone and hairline. She is in her early 30s, with shoulder-length, softly wavy honey-blonde hair that is darker at the roots and parted slightly to one side. She has light blue eyes, a small mole on the right side of her chin, natural light makeup and a calm, warm, closed-mouth smile. Do not change her age, face shape, eye colour or hair colour. Photorealistic photograph, natural skin texture, no retouched plastic look, shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm or 85mm lens, soft natural light, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
