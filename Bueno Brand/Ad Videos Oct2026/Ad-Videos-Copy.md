# Ad videos, Norwegian and Swedish, October refresh (29 September 2026)

Four videos, 1080x1080 at 30 fps, silent so they work muted in the feed. Same spec as the September card batch. Built with the real assets: FS Siena, Bueno-logo-Blue-payoff.png and the licensed Images folder. Rendered with ffmpeg, no stock substitutes.

CTA on each: Opprett kontoen din på getbueno.com (NO), Skapa ditt konto på getbueno.com (SV).

Rules applied: every on-screen line is a complete sentence, no fragments, no fear-led urgency, no em dashes, no emojis, informal "du", € before the amount, Bueno is never called a bank.

## How they were made

Rendered frame by frame with Pillow and encoded with ffmpeg, from the real brand assets in this folder. No AI video generation was involved, and there is no voiceover, because no text-to-speech or video generation tool is connected to this session. If you want voiced or avatar versions, connect HeyGen or Higgsfield and the same scripts can be run through it.

Source scripts and the full static ad packs are in `Claude outputs/Task sweep 2026-09-29/` in the Pratik Renuse folder.
