# Norwegian video ads, October 2026: three card videos and three "more than an account" videos

All six are 1080x1080 MP4 videos, 12.5 seconds, 30 fps, no sound. Each has a cover image with the same name ending in -thumbnail.jpg. CTA button on every ad: Registrer deg.

How each video runs: the card or photo and the navy logo stripe are on screen from the first frame. The headline then builds line by line, the card slides in and settles, and each supporting sentence rises in one at a time. The finished frame holds until 9.4 seconds, then it fades to the same Bueno end card on all six: the logo large on off-white, with the O behind it and two sentences.

**End card text (all six)**
Opprett kontoen din på getbueno.com/no.
Kontoen kan være klar på ti minutter.

*(English: Open your account at getbueno.com/no. Your account can be ready in ten minutes.)* Ad set: the Norwegian Register Page ad set.

The October videos cost 4 to 5 times the card ad per registration page visit. They were built on generic UI formats (chat window, checklist, timer) in a plain sans font on pale blue gradients, with the logo shrunk to a footer. Nothing in them belonged only to Bueno.

What this set uses instead, in every video:
- FS Siena for every word, the real brand font.
- The real Bueno Visa Debit card file, and the real logo lockup, large on a navy stripe.
- The "O" from the BUENO wordmark, lifted from the logo file, as the background shape on the card ads.
- Licensed Getty and iStock photos from Bueno Brand/Images (none of them used in the September card set).
- Gold only on the accent words of the headline, as the guidelines say.
- A different layout in every video: card hero, photo split, numbered list, photo-top columns, big number, and a B&W portrait with a navy block.
