# Getting happy bank customers to move to Bueno

28 September 2026. Built from the getbueno.com pages, the Bueno folder, the project docs and Notion (the 100-post Facebook groups database, the ads database, personas and customer feedback).

## What we're up against

These people aren't angry with their bank. It works, so moving feels like effort for no clear gain. The Notion research backs this up. The people who don't switch say things like "not sure switching is going to be worth the hassle". Many believe only a traditional bank is a real bank, and some simply don't want to pay for something they think is free.

So a message saying their bank is bad won't land, and our brand rules don't allow it anyway. What works better is making the cost of staying visible, and making the first step so small it doesn't feel like switching at all.

## The reasons that actually move people

1. **Help in Norwegian from a real person.** This is the number one reason real customers give. Camilla wrote that Norwegian assistance "was absolutely decisive", and it also comes first in the UGC notes on what works in ads.
2. **We move the direct debits.** Customers remember this part: Lars said "Paula helped me change my direct debit", and Agnar said "in less than a week all invoices transferred". Your 30-second recording proves it in the app.
3. **Fees they've stopped noticing.** Fees are the biggest pain in the Facebook groups, with about 25 of the 100 posts. Examples are quarterly maintenance fees and charges on incoming transfers. Most happy customers have never added theirs up.
4. **One place for everything to do with the house.** That covers the account, the card, direct debits, the tax return, the property documents and the valuation. No bank offers the property side.
5. **Nothing freezes because of paperwork nobody told you about.** Blocked accounts and document demands come up about 13 times in the Facebook groups. We can speak to calm and control here, without scaring anyone.

## What we can't say

- We can't name any bank. We can't use the old bank fee table on the website either, because the figures are from March 2023 and name the banks.
- No savings percentages (50% or 60%), star ratings or customer counts. None of them can be checked right now.
- We can't say "same day" or "within hours" about support. Support is open weekdays from 10:00 to 17:00 CET.
- On the Standard plan, direct debits are self-serve, and Bueno only helps fully on Select and Premium. So we say "you can ask us to move it", not "we move everything for you".

## New videos in this batch (same style as the 30-second one)

**NO-Gebyrsjekken.mp4 (20 seconds).** It doesn't criticise their bank. It asks them to open last year's statement, search for "comisión" and add up what they find, so they work out the cost themselves. Then it shows that Bueno costs under €2 a week, the app and the card. It ends by saying they don't have to close their bank, and can start by moving one bill.

**NO-UX-FoerOgNaa.mp4 (22 seconds).** It opens with the old way of moving a bill: phone the supplier, explain in Spanish, fill in a form, wait. Each line gets crossed out. Then the real app does it with the clock running, and it ends on "you can keep your bank and move the bills when it suits you".

## More formats worth making next

1. **Keep your bank, start with one bill.** A series of short videos, one per bill: water, electricity, community fees, insurance. Each shows that one bill being moved in the app. It needs a new screen recording per bill type.
2. **A real call with the support team.** Show a Norwegian-speaking team member picking up the phone, alongside the app. This is our strongest reason to switch, and we have no footage of it. The team would need to film 30 seconds.
3. **The customer's own words.** Put Camilla's and Lars's quotes on screen over the app. We would need their written permission first.
4. **Everything in one place.** Scroll the app from the account to direct debits, the property page, documents and the tax return, with one line of text per screen. It needs one continuous screen recording of the current app.
5. **Tax return season.** Timed for when the non-resident tax return is due. Show last year's form stored and pre-filled in the app. We would need to confirm which plans include it before running this.
