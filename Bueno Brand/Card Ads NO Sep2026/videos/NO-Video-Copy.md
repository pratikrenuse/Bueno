All four are 1080x1080 squares at 30 fps, without sound, so they work muted in the feed. CTA button on each: Registrer deg (Sign up).

Privacy: the recordings show Maria's NIE number, mobile number and address, plus a real water bill. Those areas are blurred in every frame. The home screen with transaction names is not used.
