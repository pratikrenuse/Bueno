| Referral video | 3,645 | 17 | 214 | Weakest |

What wins:
1. The card is the offer that converts. Both card announcement ads are the two cheapest in the account, in two languages.
2. The winning image is simple. It has a light background, sparkles, a navy ribbon, the real card large in the centre and the logo. There is almost no text.
3. The format is not the reason on its own. Mortgages used the same ribbon template and cost 104 NOK. The card itself is what works.
4. Real people on video (Susan, Petter) converted at about 40 NOK but never got budget.

What loses:
1. Mortgages took about 31,600 NOK, the most of any ad, at 2.6 times the cost of the card ad.
2. Curiosity ads get clicks but not signups. Customer service story (9.8% CTR) and Direct Debits copy (21% CTR) both sent traffic that did not register.
3. Copies of old ads fade. The card announcement ran at frequency 7.2 in the old Norwegian ad set before it was reused, which is why each relaunch gives one or two good weeks.

What the v2 set does: it keeps the winning structure (card as hero, ribbon, sparkles, very little text) but with new images, so it reads as fresh rather than a copy.
