# Norwegian video ads, October 2026: three new formats

Six videos are square, 1080x1080 MP4, 30 fps and silent. There are three formats, each used once for the card and once for "more than an account". Every video has a cover image with the same name ending in -thumbnail.jpg. The CTA button on all six is Registrer deg. Run them in the Norwegian Register Page ad set.

Every video ends on the same Bueno end card:
Opprett kontoen din på getbueno.com/no.
Kontoen kan være klar på ti minutter.

## The three formats

**1. The O lens (Linse).** The video opens on the Bueno logo. The other letters fade, the O lifts out of the wordmark, and the first scene opens inside it. Every scene change after that goes through the O as well. Only Bueno can use this transition, because it's built from the real wordmark.

**2. The receipt (Kvittering).** The navy Bueno stripe works as a receipt printer. The receipt prints line by line, then tears off. In the card version, the real card taps the stripe first and the receipt shows a day of card payments in Spain. In the other version, the receipt lists what Bueno handled for you during the year.

**3. The departure board (Tavle).** A split-flap board, the kind every Norwegian knows from Gardermoen, clacks through each message in FS Siena. The gold accent word lands last. It sits over a real Spanish photo, and in the card version the card rises in under the board.

Why this replaces the October videos: chat, checklist, quiz and timer are formats any brand can post, and they cost 178 to 230 NOK per Registration Page visit against 42 NOK for the card ad. These three are built only from Bueno's own things: the wordmark O, the navy stripe, the real card, FS Siena and licensed photos.
