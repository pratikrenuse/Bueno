# Norwegian video ads, October 2026: six formats

These are the six final videos, each in its own format. Three are about the card and three are about "more than an account". All six are square, 1080x1080 MP4, 30 fps and silent. Each has a cover image with the same name ending in -thumbnail.jpg. The CTA button is Registrer deg. Run them in the Norwegian Register Page ad set.

Every video ends on the same Bueno end card:

How each format works:
- **The receipt.** The real card taps the navy Bueno stripe, and the stripe prints a receipt of an ordinary day in Spain, then it tears off.
- **The departure board.** A split-flap board like the one at Gardermoen clacks through the messages over a Spanish village, and the card rises in below.
- **The postcard.** A postcard from Spain lands and flips over. A handwritten note writes itself, the real card sticks on as the postage stamp, and a Bueno postmark stamps over it.
- **The O lens.** The O lifts out of the Bueno wordmark and opens each scene inside it.
- **The Bueno seal.** Papers land on a desk one at a time. A round Bueno rubber seal, with the O at its centre and BUENO, PROPERTY SIMPLIFIED, ESPAÑA around the edge, stamps each one as the headline changes.
- **The Polaroids.** Polaroids of Spain drop onto a light blue table one by one, each with a handwritten caption, and they stack up.
