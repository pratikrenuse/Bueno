## What goes in the community

Ranked by what agents cannot get anywhere else. This is the part that
makes the link worth clicking.

**1. Straight answers on non-resident tax.**
The rules changed on 24 June 2026. Order HAC/623/2026 now requires rental
declarations to itemise gross income and deductible expenses. From tax year
2026, rental returns move to a single window of 1 to 20 April, replacing
quarterly filing. EU and EEA owners can file one pooled annual return. Very
few agents know this. Their clients will ask.

**2. The 100% tax answer, in one paragraph they can forward.**
Announced January 2025. Never voted on. Stalled in Congress, and the
government's own January 2026 housing package left it out. Agents get asked
this weekly and fumble it. Give them the calm, accurate answer.

**3. The annual tax nobody warned the buyer about.**
Non-resident owners owe imputed income tax on a property they never rent
out, filed on Modelo 210, due 31 December of the following year. It is 19%
for EU and EEA owners and 24% for everyone else, which now includes the
British. Buyers are consistently blindsided by this and the agent hears
about it first.

**4. The rental registry.**
Codes required for listings with online booking, annual return each February.
Affects every client who mentions renting the place out.

**5. Non-resident mortgage reality.**
LTV of 60 to 70%, deposits of 30 to 40%, plus 8 to 13% in acquisition costs.
Agents need this to qualify a buyer in the first conversation instead of the
fifth viewing.

**6. Running cost benchmarks by region.**
Energy, insurance, IBI, community fees. Bueno has first party data here that
no portal has. It answers the question every buyer asks: what will this
actually cost me to own?

**Deliberately excluded: general market data.** Prices, transaction volumes
and demand indexes are free from idealista/data, Spanish Property Insight,
INE and Registradores. Posting them makes the group look like a newsletter
and lowers what people think it is worth.

## Voice rule for this campaign

Write in complete, conversational sentences. Short does not mean clipped.
No fragments, no noun piles like "the tax form, the electricity bill, the
paperwork". Say what is actually happening to the agent, in the words a
person would use out loud. Read every line aloud before it ships. If you
would not say it to an agent over coffee, rewrite it.

## Sending notes

- Personalise line one with the agency's area where possible.
- One link only, at the CTA. Branded domain, not a shortener.
- Keep an unsubscribe footer. B2B email in Spain sits under LSSI, which
  expects prior consent or a prior relationship. Worth a legal check before
  the first send.
- Send Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00 to 09:30 CET.
- Have three or four real answers already posted before the first agent
  arrives. An empty group loses them in one glance.
- Re-verify the 100% tax status on the day you send. It is the hook, and it
  is a live political file.
