## 10. UI DESIGN STANDARD AND C.L.E.A.R. FRAMEWORK

Apply before designing, building, auditing or modifying any screen, page, component, form, dashboard, email, or HTML/React output.

**Output pattern for any UI deliverable:**
1. Run a C.L.E.A.R. audit and score each pillar 0 to 5 briefly (25 total).
2. Address the lowest-scoring pillar first. Re-check earlier pillars after every change.
3. Deliver the final code or copy with one unmissable primary action, benefit-led copy, Gestalt-based grouping, accessible contrast and tap targets, and a fitting emotional payoff.

**C: Copywriting.** Make the benefit obvious in 1 to 2 seconds (WIIFM, what is in it for me). Cut any sentence that breaks nothing if removed. Sound like a person, not a brochure. Specific active verbs. Add reassurance near friction points ("You can change this later"). Tests: Barstool Test (would you say this to a friend?) and Copy Swap Test (could a competitor reuse these exact words? If yes, it is too vague).

**L: Layout (Gestalt).**
- Similarity: same role, same look; reuse components, minimise variants.
- Proximity: tight spacing within groups, loose between groups.
- Simplicity: remove styling that carries no meaning; one primary action per region.
- Alignment: commit to a 4px grid; align edges, especially left edges.
- Common Region: group with cards and sections; subtle fills before heavy borders; use max-width.
- Continuity: one clear top-left to bottom scan path, no zig-zags.

**E: Emphasis.** Six dials: Size, Colour, Space, Placement, Visualisation, Motion. Commit to 1 or 2 leading dials and let the rest support. Diagnose what competes for attention before reaching for colour, since placement and space often matter more. Squint Test: blur the screen, the key element must still dominate. If everything shouts, nothing stands out.

**A: Accessibility.** Design for permanent, temporary and situational limits. Every screen: visible without searching, operable without precision, actionable without guessing. Fitts's Law: big, well-spaced tap targets. WCAG minimum 4.5:1 text contrast. Never rely on colour alone. Prevent errors: disable invalid actions, provide undo, confirm irreversible actions.

**R: Reward.** Match the payoff to the user's dominant emotion:
- Control (status, ETAs, confirmations, undo; reduces anxiety, gives safety).
- Competence (progress bars, done states, streaks; effort feels rewarded).
- Recognition (badges, roles, "verified"; pride, belonging).
30-Second Reward Test: the screen must clearly answer at least one of "Am I safe? Did I do well? Am I recognised?" Keep rewards proportional and reserve big celebration for real milestones.

**Scorecard template:**

| Pillar | Score (0 to 5) | Priority fix |
|---|---|---|
| C: Copywriting | | |
| L: Layout | | |
| E: Emphasis | | |
| A: Accessibility | | |
| R: Reward | | |
| Total | /25 | |

Re-score after changes. Ask "What would make this pillar a 5?" and "How do we raise the lowest pillar by exactly 1 point?"

UI copy guardrails: no hype, no emojis, no em dashes, direct and clear. Do not render documentation inside code blocks unless asked.

## 12. STANDING EDITORIAL RULES AND VERIFIED TAX FACTS

**John's permanent editorial rules (apply to all copy):**
- Never claim Bueno can check, verify or look up a customer's outstanding tax debt. Only the owner, or someone with a legal power of attorney visiting a Spanish tax office in person, can determine this. Bueno can only assist with filing and payment.
- Never make undocumented claims about customer behaviour (for example "most foreign owners are behind on their taxes"). Use only verifiable facts.
- Community fees in Spain are already paid by direct debit. Never position Bueno as making them "automatic" or solving a recurring payment problem.

**Additional standing content rules:**
- Avoid the ECJ ruling on inheritance and wealth tax relief for non-residents entirely. Some owners misread it as eliminating the annual non-resident property tax (Modelo 210) obligation, and writing about it perpetuates the confusion.
- The non-resident ownership registry (NRA registry) was struck down by Spain's Supreme Court in May 2026. Do not reference it as current.
- Modelo 210 cadastral rate: 2% is standard, 1.1% is the exception, not the default.
- Wealth tax: Madrid and Andalucia are exempt. Include regional nuance.
- The 3% capital gains withholding on a property sale goes directly to the tax office.

**Newsletter format rules:**
- The "Trends" or market section heading is 5 words or fewer with exactly 2 sentences of body copy. No exceptions.
- News item headings are short and punchy.
- Body copy per item: two to three short sentences maximum.
- Keep overall length tight.

**Verified tax rules used on the tax landing page (compiled 3 September 2026):**
- **Imputed income (Modelo 210), non-rented property:** cadastral value x 1.1% (if the municipality revised values within the last 10 years) or 2% (if not), taxed at 19% for EU/EEA residents and 24% for everyone else including the UK post-Brexit. Due 31 December of the year after the tax year. The 2025 return is due 31 December 2026.
- **Rental income:** see the "Read this first" list at the top for the schedule conflict. EU/EEA owners may deduct expenses. Non-EU owners are taxed on gross income.
- **Property sale:** the buyer withholds 3% and pays it to the Agencia Tributaria (AEAT). The seller declares capital gains and reclaims any excess within four months of completion.
- **Resident IRPF (Modelo 100):** filed April to June.
- **Modelo 720:** for Spanish tax residents with over 50,000 euros in any of three asset categories abroad. Filed 1 January to 31 March. After the 2022 CJEU ruling, penalties are 200 euros per category, maximum 600, and the normal 4-year statute applies.
- **Late filing:** voluntary disclosure carries a 1% to 15% surcharge plus interest beyond 12 months. If AEAT finds it first, 50% to 150% of the tax owed.
- **Statute of limitations:** 4 years.
- **Direct debit:** must be filed roughly 5 working days before the deadline.
- **Squatting (older chatbot knowledge base, verify before reuse):** police can act quickly if squatters are found within 48 hours and the owner can prove ownership; after that a court process applies, which can take 18 months to 2 years. Owners should never take matters into their own hands. Prevention includes renting the property out, alarm and automation systems, neighbours checking in, and illegal occupation insurance.
- **Holiday lettings VAT (older chatbot knowledge base, verify):** exempt unless hospitality services are provided (10%) or it is a commercial sublease (21%).

Because the tax law changes often, ask the AI to flag any date or rate it cannot confirm from a current official source.
