## Where Bueno wins

1. **Price on the entry product.** €49 sits inside the online-filer band and well under the advisory band (PTI, lawyers, gestores).
2. **Fixed quote before filing.** Lawyers and gestores commonly bill hourly. This is the strongest honest differentiator and does not require attacking anyone.
3. **Bundled.** €99 membership already carries the account, card, valuation and document vault. Tax is an add-on to a relationship, not a standalone transaction.
4. **Seven languages with human support.** Taxadora offers seven site languages but no phone-first support; appointments must be booked by email.
5. **Document vault pre-fill.** Year two is nearly automatic. Nobody else in this set owns the customer's document storage.

## Verified tax rules used on the page

- **Imputed income (Modelo 210)**: cadastral value x 1.1% (if the municipality revised values within 10 years) or 2% (if not), taxed at 19% for EU/EEA residents, 24% for everyone else including the UK post-Brexit. Due 31 December of the year following the tax year. 2025 return due 31 December 2026.
- **Rental income**: annual since the 2024 tax year, filed 1 to 20 April of the following year. Zero-result returns stay 1 to 20 January. EU/EEA owners may deduct expenses; non-EU owners are taxed on gross.
- **Property sale**: buyer withholds 3% and pays it to AEAT. Seller declares capital gains and reclaims any excess within four months of completion.
- **Resident IRPF (Modelo 100)**: filed April to June.
- **Modelo 720**: Spanish tax residents with over €50,000 in any of three asset categories abroad. Filed 1 January to 31 March. Post-2022 CJEU ruling the penalties are €200 per category, max €600, and a normal 4-year statute applies.
- **Late filing**: voluntary disclosure carries a 1% to 15% surcharge plus interest beyond 12 months. If AEAT finds it first, 50% to 150% of tax owed.
- **Statute of limitations**: 4 years.
- **Direct debit**: must be filed roughly 5 working days before the deadline.
