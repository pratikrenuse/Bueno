# Instagram personal-profile personas

Read this before creating any Instagram post for 24/7 Spain / Bueno.

## The idea

Instagram content does not go out as a company or branded page. It goes out from
**personal profiles**, one account per language. Each post is written as if a real
person is sharing it from her own account: first person, natural, conversational.
It is not written in a brand voice and it does not read like an ad.

## Three personas, three folders. NEVER mix them.

There are three different women, and each one covers two languages. A post may only
use photos from the folder for its own language. Never use a photo from another
persona's folder, and never combine two personas in one post or carousel. Never use
stock photos, other people or generated lookalikes.

## Voice and content (decided 8 Nov 2026)

- Each persona is an expat who lives in Spain full-time. She shares property and
  everyday-life tips in the first person, as things she has learned living there.
- Every post is pure value. **Bueno is never mentioned**: no name, no link, no product.
- Content comes from idealista insights (see `NEWS_RUNBOOK.md` and the idealista
  digest) plus Bueno's own data, but the posts never mention Bueno.

## Post formats (rotate across each feed)

1. **Photo + long caption:** one photo of the persona, with the information carried in
   the caption.
2. **Carousel:** a photo of the persona on the first slide, followed by information
   slides.

Rotate the two formats, and rotate layouts inside carousels too (see the
layout-variety rule).

## Rules that still apply
