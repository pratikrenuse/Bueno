## Image format: square, and why the deck needed a change

The cards are 1080x1080. LinkedIn accepts 1200x627 landscape, 1080x1080 square and
1080x1350 portrait for a native image post, and square occupies more vertical feed space
than 1.91:1, so square is the stronger choice for the team's posts.

The deck, however, was built entirely around 1.91:1, because every Pexels image came
through `api/_lk_images.js` with `w=1200&h=630`. So a square card was being cropped to a
340px band, losing the top of the headline and the whole brand bar. Two fixes in
`internal-linkedin/index.jsx`:

- The post preview was `maxHeight: 340, objectFit: 'cover'`. Now `aspectRatio: '1 / 1'`
  with `objectFit: 'contain'` and an off-white ground. Do not put that maxHeight back.
- The alternates thumbnails were boxed at `aspectRatio: '1200 / 630'` and rewrote the URL
  with a Pexels-only `w=1200&h=630` replace, which is a no-op on a `/posts/` path. Now a
  1:1 box, and the rewrite only runs on Pexels URLs.

`api/_lk_images.js` is untouched: it still serves 1200x630 Pexels URLs, which is correct
for the alternates it generates. Both shapes now display without cropping.
