# Spain 24/7 LinkedIn programme: what changed, 17 September 2026

Read this before committing. Everything below is already on disk or in the database.

## The live images carry no text

The 105 posts go out as a licensed photograph under the Bueno lockup. Nothing else on
the picture. One file serves all six languages, which is the whole reason: copy burned
into an image cannot be translated, so a Norwegian post would have gone out with an
English card over it. The text cards that were built first are kept, not deleted, see
"The text cards are archived" below.

## Modified files

- `api/_lk_images.js` — each post's frame is now the first entry in its `image_options`,
  and the default. This matters: `_lk_refresh.js` rewrites `image_url` whenever the stored
  value is not in `image_options`, so without this a Sync would have swapped all 105 posts
  back to a Pexels photograph. The Pexels alternates are untouched and still switchable in
  the deck.
- `internal-linkedin/index.jsx` — the deck was cropping square images. The preview box and
  the alternates thumbnails are now 1:1 and `contain`, not `cover` with a 340px cap.
- `studio/themes/247spain.json` — disclaimer line removed from the footer. This is the
  PUBLIC theme, so "General information, not tax advice." no longer appears on
  247spain.es images either. Put it back if that line was doing compliance work.
- `TRANSLATIONS.md` — Norwegian Currencies Direct terms and four log entries.

`studio/renderer/simple3.py` is untouched and still works. `tpl.py` imports from it.

## Why the photo pool is 15 and not 22

Seven files came out of it, and the first render showed why they had to:

- six were byte-level duplicates of other files under different names, which is what
  produced repeated frames (`marina.jpg` is not a marina, it is the hat by the pool)
- one was greyscale, and read as off-brand against a warm set

Of each duplicate pair the higher resolution original was kept, so three photos gained
resolution in the process. Nothing was thrown away, and `studio/PHOTO_POOL.md` lists every exclusion with its reason. Fifteen photos over 105 posts is seven reuses each, so the renderer varies
focal point and zoom per post, scores every candidate crop for detail so none lands on
empty sky, and spaces a photo's reuses at least six posts apart inside a deck. One shot,
a hazy infinity pool, only has five usable crops and is used five times rather than seven.
`studio/check_frames.py` enforces all of that, plus that the lockup is present and
uncropped. If you add photos, run it first.

## The images will look broken until you deploy

`image_url` is a site-relative path. `api/_email.js` `imageSrc()` prefixes
`https://www.247spain.es` for email, and the deck resolves it against its own host. Neither
works until `public/posts/` is deployed. So: commit, let Vercel build, then open
`/internal-linkedin`.
