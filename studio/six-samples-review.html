Owners, day 2 irnr_who_pays band_rules pending

Fixed: Quarterly Modelo 210 removed. Base is cadastral value, not market. Q3 2026 deadline added.

Own a place in Spain and leave it empty most of the year? You still file a return on it. Spain taxes the use of the home, not only the rent.

The base is the cadastral value, not what you paid for it. Take the figure off your IBI receipt. Cadastral values sit well below market, which is why the bill is usually smaller than people brace for.

The maths is cadastral value times 1.1 percent or 2 percent, then 19 percent if you are in the EU, 24 percent if you are not. On a cadastral value of 300,000 euros at 1.1 percent, an owner in Norway pays 19 percent of 3,300 euros, about 627 a year.

The date is 31 December, for the year before.

One change still trips people up. Rental income is filed annually now, not quarterly. The last quarterly rental filing is Q3 2026, due 1 to 20 October 2026.

Owners, day 3 squatter_eviction quote_band pending

Fixed: Replaced. The 48-hour rule is a myth, so the post now teaches the actual test.

There is no 48-hour rule in Spain. It gets repeated constantly and it is not in any statute.

What matters is not a clock but a test: clear signs an offence was committed, urgency, and proportionality. That is what lets police act at the door rather than sending you to court.

Since April 2025 there is also a fast-track route, which lists a trial within fifteen days.

So the practical question is not how fast you call. It is what the situation looks like when someone arrives. Signs of forced entry, an alarm log, a neighbour who can say the place was occupied last week. That evidence is what makes urgency arguable.

If anyone sells you a service on the strength of a 48-hour window, that is the moment to ask what they are actually referring to.

Owners, day 7 wealth_tax number_hero approved

Fixed: SURGICAL EDIT to the approved reviewer text: 2.5 percent corrected to 3.5, plus the non-resident discount caveat. Everything else is word for word theirs. Needs re-approval.

Most owners will never meet Spain's wealth tax. If your Spanish assets are drifting toward 700,000 euros, you're not most owners, so you should know exactly where the line sits.

The tax applies to residents and non-residents alike, measured on total assets. Below 700,000 euros, nothing is owed. Above it, the tax bites incrementally, only on the value over the line, at progressive rates from 0.2 to 3.5 percent. No sale, no income, no trigger. Owning is enough.

Where you own matters as much as what you own, though, because the tax is shaped by local government. Madrid and Andalucia apply a 100 percent discount, so identical assets there mean a zero bill. That discount is not automatic for a non-resident: taxed under state rules, no regional bonificacion applies unless you opt in to the region. Residents who live in their Spanish home also get an extra 300,000 euro allowance on that main home (can vary between regions). If you're a non-resident, that allowance isn't yours.

Owners, day 15 plusvalia timeline pending

Fixed: CGT is 19 percent for everyone, not 19/24. Plusvalia is 30 working days, six months on an inheritance.

Two numbers people get wrong when they sell in Spain.

The gain. Capital gains on sale are 19 percent for everyone. There is no separate higher rate for non-EU sellers on this one, and that assumption costs sellers real money in the negotiation.

The plusvalia. You have 30 working days from the date of the deed. Working days, not calendar days, which in practice is closer to six weeks than to one month.

If the property came to you through an inheritance, that same plusvalia runs six months instead.

Put both dates in your calendar on the day you sign, not the week the deadline lands.

Owners, day 16 buying_fees compare pending

Fixed: Madrid 6, Andalucia 7, Valencia 9 since June 2026. Catalonia and Pais Vasco left out, both blocked in the rules base.

People budget for a Spanish resale as if there is one national rate. There is not. Your region sets it.

Madrid charges 6 percent. Andalucia charges 7. Valencia moved to 9 in June 2026.

On a 300,000 euro flat that spread is 9,000 euros, decided by which side of a regional line the property sits on.

One more thing before you fix a budget. New builds are taxed differently from resales, so a figure quoted for one tells you nothing about the other.

Check the rate for your specific region, and check it for the year you are buying in.

Agents, day 23 ag_nie_timing checklist pending

Fixed: Form is EX-15 not EX-18. Fee is 9.84 euros, not 11.

Two details send NIE applications back, and both are easy to get right.

The form is the EX-15, filled in Spanish, submitted in two copies. Not the EX-18. The EX-18 is EU citizen registration, a different form for a different purpose, and it is the most common mix-up.

The fee is 9.84 euros, paid first at a bank branch on form 790 codigo 012. The stamped proof of payment goes in with the application, not after it.

Get both right and the appointment is short. Get either wrong and your buyer loses the slot and waits for the next one, which is where timelines actually slip.
