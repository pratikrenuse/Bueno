# LinkedIn programme: state of play, 18 September 2026

This supersedes `HANDOVER_2026-09-17.md`. Everything below is done, in the repo or in the
database, and verified. Commit and deploy.

## What the programme is

105 posts. Six languages each, 630 rows in `linkedin_posts`. Every post goes out as a
licensed photograph under the Bueno lockup with no text on the image, which is why one
image serves all six languages. The caption is translated; the picture never needs to be.

## Images

- `public/posts/<slug>.jpg`, 105 files, 1080x1080, 13.4 MB. Live.
- Four brand layouts, so the same photograph never reads as the same picture twice:
  `full` (photo to the edges, navy bar at the base), `inset` (photo on the off-white field,
  navy lockup, gold rule), `block` (navy block down the left, gold hairline, photo filling
  the rest), `panel` (photo above, light blue panel, navy bar). 30 / 30 / 30 / 15.
- Palette is the brand's own: navy #010221, light blue #CBEFFF, gold #C9A96E,
  off-white #F8F7F4. Nothing else in the non-photographic areas.
- The lockup is pasted whole, every time. Never crop it, never re-typeset PROPERTY
  SIMPLIFIED. If it does not fit, widen the panel.
- Rendered by `studio/render_frames.py`, checked by `studio/check_frames.py`, which fails
  on a duplicate or greyscale photo in the pool, a crop that landed on empty sky, a cropped
  or missing lockup, an off-brand colour in a flat area, and any two of the 105 images
  being near-identical. It passes clean.

## The photo pool, and the one real limit

Fifteen unique licensed photographs, listed in `studio/PHOTO_POOL.md`. That is everything
licensed on this machine: `studio/photos` held ten, the Images folder thirteen more, six of
those were byte-level duplicates under different names, and one was greyscale.

More stock photography is the single best improvement available, and the one thing that
could not be done from here: neither the cloud container nor the desktop can reach Pexels,
Unsplash or any other image host, so nothing new could be downloaded and composited.
Fifteen photographs over 105 posts is seven uses each, handled by four layouts, varied
crops and zooms, and a rule that keeps a photograph from returning within six posts of
itself inside a deck. Drop more files into `studio/photos-brand`, re-run the two scripts,
and the reuse count falls immediately.

## Translations, all 630 rows

- 105 English masters, each carrying the English CTA line.
- 525 translated rows: Norwegian, Swedish, Dutch, French, Spanish, 105 each.
- Made from the approved English against the glossary and locked terms in
  `TRANSLATIONS.md` and the rules in `api/_translate.js`: figures, dates, deadlines, law
  names and worked examples preserved exactly, number formatting localised, the hashtag
  line left in English, the official per-language CTA in place of the English one, no
  dashes, no emoji, Bueno never described as a bank, no competitor named.
- `source_hash` on every translated row is the sha1 of its English master as stored, so
  `/api/linkedin-translate` sees all 525 as current and will not regenerate them. Edit one
  English post and only that post's five translations go stale.
- Figures were checked row by row against the master in the database: no row lost a figure.
  The only five rows with one digit fewer are the Spanish ones that write "tercer trimestre
  de 2026" where the English writes "Q3 2026".

## The source modules now match the table

`api/_linkedin_translations.js` holds all 525 translations, and
`api/_linkedin_batch1.js`, `api/_linkedin_agents.js` and `api/_linkedin_attorneys.js` hold
the 105 English masters as approved, regenerated from the live table.

This matters more than it sounds. `/api/linkedin-refresh` writes these modules into
Supabase, and it rewrites any row that is pending or approved and not hand-edited. Before
this, a Sync would have pushed the older text back over 150 translated rows. A full Sync was
simulated offline against the live table, row by row, as the last step: 525 translation rows
`no_change`, 105 English rows `skipped_edited`. Pressing Sync changes nothing.

It also closes the nine re-pointed partner slugs recorded in
`claude/LinkedIn_CTA_In_Post.md`: the module and the table now hold the same post under
each of those slugs, so the interim guard in `_lk_refresh.js` has nothing left to guard.

## One thing that is a decision, not a defect

`team_members` holds one active member per language, all six with `stream = owners`.
`/api/linkedin-dispatch` walks the three streams and reports
`no members with an email on this stream` for agents and attorneys, which is accurate
rather than broken: those 45 posts are written, translated and imaged, and they wait for
someone to be assigned to those streams. The owners stream sends as normal.
