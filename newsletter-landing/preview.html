This fortnight in Spanish property

The Bueno Newsletter

It has been a big fortnight. Prices hit a new record, and the Supreme Court ruled on a community fee that many owners will recognise.

News

Used home prices hit an all-time record.

Used homes averaged 2,955 euros per square metre in October, the highest idealista has ever recorded.

News

The Supreme Court strikes down a 50 cent a day late fee.

Communities can still chase unpaid fees, but their penalties have to stay reasonable.
